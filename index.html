The Problem

Complexity of building installations
and user demands are out of reach
for human execution.

Buildings generate lots of data, but it's scattered across different systems and vendors, hard to extract and nearly impossible to make sense of. Users of buildings have almost daily alternating demands. Teams can't catch up — instead they manage reactively, only finding out about issues when a complaint arrives or something breaks.
